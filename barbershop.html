No. 03The Shop

A neighborhood shop with old habits.

Glen & Blade Barbers is a four-chair shop on Sample Street where the clippers are sharp, the towels are hot and the radio stays low. We cut North Shore regulars, their kids, and the occasional groom who left it until the morning of.

We take our time, we remember how you like it, and we keep our opinions about your fantasy team to ourselves. The coffee’s on, the game is on mute, and walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead just guarantees the chair.
