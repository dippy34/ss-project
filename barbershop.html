On Sample Street since 2009.

Marco opened the shop in an old shoe-repair storefront, kept the tin ceiling, and spent that first winter rebuilding four cast-iron chairs in his garage. They’re still the only chairs we use.

The radio stays on the ballgame with the volume low. The coffee is free and stronger than it needs to be. We cut North Shore regulars, their kids, and the occasional groom who left it until the morning of.

Come in, take a number, sit down. Nobody here is going to try to sell you a pomade.

House Rules

Pick a chair.

Four barbers, four chairs. Ask for someone by name or take whoever’s free. Everyone here can do everything on the cards.
