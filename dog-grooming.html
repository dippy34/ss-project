Every dog goes home with a report card.

Wag & Wash Studio is a small, appointment-only grooming studio in Glenview. It’s just us, the tubs and your dog, so we can take our time and tell you exactly how it went.

  • One dog at a time.Your dog is the only one in the studio. No crates, no barking neighbors, no waiting in a kennel for pickup.
  • Low-stress handling.Our groomers are trained in fear-free, low-stress techniques and certified in pet first aid & CPR.
  • No sedation, no rushing.If a dog needs a break, we take one. If a cut isn’t safe today, we call you and explain why.

House rules

  1. Rabies proof, please.Every dog needs a current rabies vaccination. Bring the certificate or have your vet email it before drop-off.
  2. Our matting policy.We brush out what’s comfortable for your dog. Severe mats are shaved short instead; we always call first, and dematting is $10–$30.
  3. One dog at a time.Booking two? We’ll schedule them back to back.
  4. Pickup.We text when your dog is ready. Please pick up within 30 minutes.
  5. Changes.Give us 24 hours’ notice to reschedule or cancel.
