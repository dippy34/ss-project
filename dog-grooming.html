About us

A calm, cozy studio built around your dog

Wag & Wash Studio is a small, appointment-only grooming studio tucked into a sunny corner of Glenview. We started with one simple idea: grooming should feel like a spa day, not a scary day.

Our groomers are certified in fear-free handling and pet first aid. We go at your dog’s pace with quiet dryers, non-slip tables and plenty of treat breaks, so nervous pups and senior sweeties leave relaxed (and ridiculously soft).

  • One-on-one grooming, never crated
  • Fear-free certified & pet first-aid trained
  • Gentle, hypoallergenic, tearless products
  • A “glamour shot” texted to you before pickup
