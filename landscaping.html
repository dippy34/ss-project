Snow season, in writing

Every snow customer gets these four rules on paper before the first storm.

  1. 2"

    We plow at two inches

    Measured at the shop. Lighter dustings get salted instead, free for contract customers.

  2. 6:00

    Done before you leave

    Overnight snow is off your drive by 6 a.m. If it keeps falling, we keep coming.

  3. +1

    We come back for the curb

    The slush wall the village plow leaves at the end of your drive is part of the job.

  4. Nov

    Stakes go in early

    Reflective markers along your drive and beds in November, so the blade never finds your lawn.

    Sixteen seasons from one Glenview shop

    Northshore Green Co. started in 2009 with six lawns on one street, a used push mower and a borrowed pickup.
