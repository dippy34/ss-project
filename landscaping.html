What we do

Yard care for every season, snowstorms included

From the first spring cleanup to the last 5 a.m. plow, one local crew handles it all. Pick a season to see what we do.

Spring Mar–May

In season now

Wake the yard up right after a long Illinois winter.

  • Spring Cleanup

    We clear out winter's leftovers (matted leaves, fallen branches, plow-scraped turf) then edge the beds and give the lawn its first cut.

    • Debris hauled away
    • Plow damage patched
    • First mow & edge

  • Mulching & Garden Beds

    Crisp hand-cut bed edges, weeds pulled by the root, and a fresh 2–3" layer of hardwood mulch that holds moisture all summer.

    • Choice of mulch color
    • Annuals & perennials planted
    • Bed redesigns on request

  • Shrub & Hedge Pruning

    Shaping before new growth kicks in keeps hedges full and healthy instead of leggy, and keeps walkways clear.

    • Hand-shaped, not hacked
    • Clippings cleaned up
    • Touch-ups in July

Fall Sep–Nov

In season now

Leaves down, lawn tucked in, driveway ready for the plow.

  • Fall Cleanup & Leaf Removal

    We rake, blow and haul away every last leaf (beds and gutters too, if you like) so your lawn isn't smothered all winter.

    • Hauled away, no curb piles
    • One visit or weekly to Dec
    • Gutter clearing on request

  • Aeration & Overseeding

    Fall is the best time to fix thin, tired turf. We core-aerate and overseed so it comes back thick and green in spring.

    • Core aeration
    • Shade & sun seed blends
    • Starter feed included

  • Winter Prep & Final Mow

    A short final cut, perennials trimmed back, tender shrubs wrapped in burlap, and plow stakes set along your drive.

    • Burlap wrap for evergreens
    • Hoses & spigots shut down
    • Plow markers installed

About us

A family crew that lives right here

Northshore Green Co. started in 2009 with one truck, one mower and a few neighbors on our own Glenview street. We're still family-run, and still mowing some of those very same lawns.

Today we're a small crew of nine. We're fully licensed and insured, we pay our people fairly, and we keep our routes tight so the same faces show up at your place every week. It's the kind of service you'd trust with a gate code, or your house keys.
