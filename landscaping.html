Sixteen seasons from one Glenview shop

Northshore Green Co. started in 2009 with six lawns on one street, a used push mower and a borrowed pickup. Today there are nine of us and three plow trucks, and every route stays within about six miles of the shop. That's how a 3 a.m. snowfall is gone before your alarm.

You'll see the same crew lead every week. They learn which gate sticks, where the sprinkler heads hide, and that your dog is friendly.
