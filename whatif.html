⚔️ WHAT IF ⚔️

Lead the North to Victory

Stage 1

The Virginia Decision

President Lincoln asks you to lead the North. Your home is in Virginia, but you stay loyal to the Union. What is your first big move?

Option A: The Defensive Fort
You stay in Washington D.C. to build massive walls and wait for the South to attack you first.
Option B: The Training Plan
You spend three months training your soldiers to be organized and disciplined before you march into battle.
Stage 2

Cutting the Supply Lines

The South is using the Mississippi River to send food and weapons from the West to their main army in the East.

Option A: The River Siege
You use your ships and soldiers to capture the forts along the Mississippi River to "split the South in two."
Option B: The Woods Chase
You take your soldiers into the deep Southern swamps to hunt down every small group of rebels one by one.
Stage 3

The European Threat

As shown in your map, the war is huge. England and France are thinking about helping the South so they can get more cotton.

Option A: The Big Threat
You tell the European kings that you will sink their ships if they even look at a Southern port.
Option B: The Anti-Slavery Proclamation
You convince Lincoln to announce that a Union victory means all slaves will be free forever.
Stage 4

Breaking the Economy

You are deep in Southern territory. You need to stop the South from being able to fight a long war.

Option A: The Railroad Raid
You focus your attacks on capturing the train hubs and iron factories that keep the South's army moving.
Option B: The Coastal Retreat
You decide the South is too big, so you move your army back to the coast and hope the blockade does all the work.
Stage 5

The Final Peace

The South is finally ready to stop fighting. You have to decide how the war officially ends.

Option A: The Peace Treaty
You allow the Southern soldiers to keep their horses and go home to their farms if they promise to never fight the Union again.
Option B: The Victory Tax
You demand that every Southern family pays a huge amount of money to the North to pay for the war.
